# Tiny Jasmini — Art Archive

> A preservation archive for artwork of my original character, **Tiny Jasmini**.

---

## What this repository is

This folder is an **archive**, not a gallery, not a portfolio, and not a stock of free assets.

Every image stored here is artwork of my original character, **Tiny Jasmini**. The collection exists for one reason: **preservation**. I want to keep a safe, permanent copy of every drawing and gift related to her, together with proper credit to the person who made it.

## What is NOT here

**The only artwork published here is artwork where I am the end user.** That means personal commissions and gifts, delivered to me, and kept by me.

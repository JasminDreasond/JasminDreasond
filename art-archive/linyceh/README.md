## Origin

Linyceh is a fictional character inspired by a real person: the JasminDreasond's sister.
Her design choices were made as a tribute, not as a biography.

---

## The Name

The name **Linyceh** is a direct representation of the original name **Aline**.
The spelling was changed for aesthetic and fictional purposes, but the connection
to the original name is intentional and permanent.

---

## Color Palette

Every color used in Linyceh's design is one of her real-life counterpart's
**favorite colors**. The palette is a personal tribute, not a random aesthetic choice.

---

## Crafting & Personality — A Snapshot in Time

Linyceh's personality and her interest in **crafting** are a snapshot of the
JasminDreasond's sister's life at the time the character was created (October 2016).

This is important:

> The character reflects what she enjoyed **at that moment**, not necessarily
> what she enjoys today.

In other words, the crafting hobby and personality traits are a **time capsule**.
They must not be treated as an up-to-date description of a real person. People
change, and this character is allowed to stay exactly where she was created.

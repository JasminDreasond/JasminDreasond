# Others arts by JasminDreasond

This is a collection of my personal artwork. It works as a gallery of studies,
experiments, and finished pieces made by hand (or by hand on a digital tablet).

The subject is intentionally open: you will find random objects, everyday items,
and anything else that caught my attention long enough to be drawn.

## What you'll find here

- **Random objects** — mugs, bottles, chairs, tools, and other ordinary things
  that are surprisingly fun to draw.
- **Studies and sketches** — quick practices focused on shape, light, and
  proportion.
- **Finished pieces** — the ones that actually turned out well.
- **Experiments** — new techniques, textures, and materials I wanted to test.
